# With Us

A recovery plan for the new mother that the whole household carries.

## The Unique Kernel
A weekly plan that puts the mother's recovery first and shares baby and house work fairly against what each person says they can carry, while keeping everyone's reasons private. Three rules make it With Us and not a chore app:

1. **The mother gets her space.** Her rest windows are hard constraints and her planned load stays below what she states.
2. **The maths is never left to AI.** Assignment and fairness are deterministic code. People set their own capacity and limits.
3. **Your reasons never leave you.** Reasons, assistant instructions, proposals, declines and private chats are visible only to their owner. The household sees only the published plan.

## Who It's For
A household in the weeks after a baby arrives: the recovering mother (Meera in the demo), her partner (Karan), and other family or helpers (her father Ramesh, Karan's mother Sunita). Today the mother quietly does it all, the partner misses appointments he hasn't found the words to explain, and nobody has a fair, shared picture of who is covering what.

## The Core Loop
Each person privately tells With Us what they can take on (hours a week, blocked days, blocked work kinds, an optional private reason). A doctor's discharge sheet is read by AI into recovery quests that a person confirms. The engine builds a fair week; each person accepts or declines what they're asked, and a decline silently re-balances the plan. The mother opens a calm quest map of small steps from her doctor and taps "I did it". Everyone comes back to see the current plan, answer what needs them, and ask their private assistant about their own week.

## Inspiration & Identity
Calm, warm and gentle. Warm cream background, white cards, baby pink surfaces with a deeper rose for actions. Source Sans 3. The mother never sees percentages, health numbers, streaks or red. A small bunny hops into a jar when she finishes a quest.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Tons of mothers are overloaded alone by responsibilities after the birth of a baby, which at times leads to postpartum depression. Even when people are available, everyone wants to help at the same time, which is also overwhelming to manage. And someone who can't help feels guilty saying no. "We want to bring a structure to the chaos and make recovery easier and less stressful for the mother, and also make it easier for people to say no when they can't help out and balance the load out."

## What "Working" Looks Like
The seed household runs locally end to end and shows in about two minutes on screen:
- Upload the sample discharge sheet; a person confirms each line beside the doctor's original words.
- Karan types his reason into the locked box; "What your household sees" shows the limit, never the reason.
- Meera's quest map: tap the walk, the bunny hops into the jar, "Your week is covered".
- Ramesh accepts one task and declines another; Sunita sees only "Plan updated", no name.
- Turning on the "baby has a cold" variant raises a shortfall card with three choices.
- **The "oh, that's cool" beat:** Ramesh asks his assistant why Karan is never free, gets a kind refusal that leaks nothing, then the real leak-test score is shown.

## The POC Boundary
- Household code plus member ID sign-in, four seeded members, roles mother, partner, other.
- Fair-plan engine with hard constraints, local search and tests.
- Discharge sheet reader with a confirm screen; recovery quests on the quest map.
- Private intake (form plus optional AI reader), consent flow with silent declines, shortfall decisions.
- Per-viewer privacy projection, output guard, leak evaluation script with a real score.
- Personal assistant and "Say it for me", with non-AI fallbacks for every AI call.
- Dashboard, Needs you, Assistant, Control panel, Landing, baby screen, dark mode. Desktop width only.
